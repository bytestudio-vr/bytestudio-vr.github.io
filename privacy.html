Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to the ByteStudio Remote Desktop application for Meta Quest (package com.bytestudio.linuxdesktop), published by ByteStudio VR ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles and why. Analytics on this product website are described separately in section 12 and are not part of the app.

In short, for the app: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data, and we operate no server. The app has no account of its own, no analytics and no advertising. It connects only to the computer that you choose, and everything it remembers stays on your headset. One exception, described in section 6: to check that the optional Ultra add-on was purchased, the app asks Horizon OS, on the headset, for the purchases of the Meta account you are signed in with. That involves your Meta user ID, which the platform uses on the device; it is never sent to us and we keep no copy of it.

1. Who is responsible

ByteStudio VR is the publisher of the app and the entity responsible for the small amount of Meta account data described in section 6. We hold no database and no server: the only personal data involved is read on your headset, by the platform, for the purchase check. You can contact us at the address given in section 10 for any question or request about that data.

2. Information that stays on your headset

To make reconnecting easier, the app stores the following information locally, in its private storage:

DataPurposeDetails
Host address, port, username Pre-fill the connection form Stored in plain form in the app's private storage.
Password Reconnect without retyping Stored only if you tick "Remember password". Encrypted (AES-GCM) with a non-exportable key of the Android Keystore. Untick the box and reconnect to erase it.
Server certificate fingerprint Detect a server impersonation (trust on first use, like SSH) Stored as a SHA-256 hash, per host and port.

This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app, and the app's data is excluded from Android backups.

3. Information sent to your own computer

The purpose of the app is to control a computer you own or administer. While connected, the app sends to that computer, over the Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP, encrypted with TLS and authenticated with NLA):

  • your credentials (to log in),
  • your pointer position and clicks,
  • your keystrokes (physical or virtual keyboard).

It receives the screen image and the audio of that computer. This traffic goes directly between your headset and your computer, usually on your local network. It does not pass through any server operated by us. What your computer does with this information is under your control.

4. Permissions used

  • Network (Internet): required to open the RDP connection to your computer.
  • Hand tracking: lets you point and click with your hands. Hand data is processed on the headset by the Meta system to drive the pointer; the app does not record it, store it or transmit it.
  • Passthrough: displays the room around the desktop. Camera images are handled by the Meta system; the app has no access to them and does not store them.
  • Rendering the controllers: the app displays the controller models the system provides, so that they are visible in the scene. No button or motion data from them leaves the headset.
  • Audio playback: the app plays the sound of the remote computer through its own audio stream. The volume controls in the app change only that stream, through a gain applied inside the app; they never change the headset's system volume, and no permission is needed for them.

The app does not use the microphone, the contacts, the location or the files of your headset.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use cookies. We do not build profiles and we do not sell data.

6. Meta platform, in-app purchase and your Meta user ID

The app runs on Meta Horizon OS and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta processes data about your use of the headset and of the store according to its own policies, which are independent of this app. See the Meta Privacy Policy and the Meta Quest Supplemental Privacy Policy.

The Ultra edition is a one-time in-app purchase, sold as an add-on through the Meta Horizon Store. Payment is handled entirely by Meta: the app never sees, stores or transmits your payment details, your card or your billing address.

To know whether Ultra was purchased, the app must ask which purchases belong to the Meta account that is signed in on the headset. It does so with the Horizon Platform SDK, which Horizon OS provides on the device. That check is the only reason the app touches account data, and it works like this:

QuestionAnswer
What data is involved Your Meta user ID, the numeric identifier of the Meta account signed in on the headset, and the list of purchases attached to that account (in practice, whether the Ultra add-on is in it). The app does not read your name, username, profile picture, email address, friends, activity or any other account data.
How it is collected On the headset, through the Horizon Platform SDK built into Horizon OS, when the app starts and when it refreshes the purchase state. Nothing is typed, no form is filled in, and no additional permission prompt is involved: it uses the Meta account already signed in on the device.
How it is used Solely to decide whether the Ultra features unlock, and to remember that answer for the next start. The user ID is used to identify the account across sessions so that a purchase follows the account that made it. It is never used for advertising, analytics, profiling, personalisation or any other purpose.
Where it is processed Inside the app's process on your headset, and by Meta's purchase service. It is never sent to us: we operate no server, and the app has nowhere to send it. It is not shared with anyone else either.
How long it is kept We keep no copy, at any time. The app stores only a yes/no flag, "Ultra unlocked", in its private storage. That flag contains no identifier, is not tied to a particular account, and is corrected at the next start if the answer changed. Meta keeps the purchase record on its side, under its own policies.

If you never buy Ultra, the check still runs when the app starts, because the app has to know whether the features are unlocked. Nothing is created or kept from it beyond the flag described above. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that flag; see section 8.

7. Sharing and retention

We share nothing, because we receive nothing. The Meta user ID and the list of purchases stay between your headset and Meta's purchase service; we hold no copy of them, and we do not sell, rent or transfer them. Locally stored connection information is kept until you remove it, as described below.

We delete user data without undue delay when you ask us to, when it is no longer needed for the purchase check, or when Meta or applicable law requires it. In practice there is nothing on our side to delete, and every request is answered in writing, as described in section 8.

8. Your choices, and deleting your data

Data deletion request. Write to bytestudio.rdp@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request. This address is our dedicated way to ask for deletion or correction of your data. We answer within 30 days, and we confirm in writing what was deleted and what was not.

Because the app sends us nothing, a deletion request usually ends with this answer: we hold no record of you, and there is nothing to erase on our side. The request is still the right way to make us check and confirm it, and to exercise your rights if you believe otherwise.

On your headset, you can also delete everything the app stores, at any time:

  • Untick Remember password and reconnect to delete the saved password.
  • Clear the app's data from the headset settings (Settings → Apps → ByteStudio Remote Desktop → Clear data) to erase the host, username, password, certificate fingerprints and the "Ultra unlocked" flag.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the headset.
  • Deleting your Meta account, or the purchase, is handled by Meta in your Meta account settings; the app loses access to the purchase check as soon as the account is gone.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your rights under the GDPR remain intact: you may ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and you may object to its processing. Use the address above; we answer within one month.

9. Children

The app is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

10. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to ask for deletion or correction of your data: bytestudio.rdp@gmail.com. For a deletion request, use the subject line Data deletion request.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, we will update this page and its "last updated" date before the new version is released.

12. Product website analytics

When enabled, this website uses Umami Cloud to measure page views, visits, referral sources, browser and device types, approximate location, and clicks on the Meta Horizon Store and APK links. A click does not tell us whether you installed the app or bought Ultra. Website analytics do not change the app's data handling.

Umami hosts the analytics data. It does not use tracking cookies or track visitors across websites. We do not send names, email addresses or app connection information to it. As with any web request, the service receives technical information such as an IP address and browser headers to process the request. The tracker excludes the current page's query string and URL fragment. The free plan retains analytics data for six months. See Umami's Privacy Policy for more information.

Analytics are disabled in local previews and when your browser sends an enabled Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. You can also block the analytics script with your browser's content blocker. For questions about website analytics, contact us using the address in section 10.
