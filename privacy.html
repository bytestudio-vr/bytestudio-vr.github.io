Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to the ByteStudio Remote Desktop application for Meta Quest (package com.bytestudio.linuxdesktop), published by ByteStudio VR ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles and why. Analytics on this product website are described separately in section 12 and are not part of the app.

In short, for the app: the app processes connection information, remote desktop content and input to connect your headset to the computer that you choose. Saved connection information stays in the app's private storage on your headset. We operate no app backend and do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or analytics. The Ultra platform feature request covers the collection and processing of your numeric Meta user ID (USER_ID) and this app's purchase records (IAP) through the Horizon Platform SDK solely to verify ownership, unlock Ultra, restore access and refresh the local unlock state. Section 6 sets out the data-handling commitments for these requested features. Local processing by the app is still processing of personal data, even without a publisher-operated server. All users can request data deletion as described in section 8.

1. Who is responsible

Cédric Guillot, based in France, publishes the app under the ByteStudio VR name (Meta developer team: byte_studio) and is the data controller responsible for the purposes and means of the app's processing, including the Meta user ID and purchase data described in section 6. Processing on your headset does not remove this responsibility. Contact details and the data deletion procedure are provided in sections 8 and 10.

2. Information that stays on your headset

To make reconnecting easier, the app stores the following information locally, in its private storage:

DataPurposeDetails
Host address, port, username Pre-fill the connection form Stored in plain form in the app's private storage.
Password Reconnect without retyping Stored only if you tick "Remember password". Encrypted (AES-GCM) with a non-exportable key of the Android Keystore. Untick the box and reconnect to erase it.
Server certificate fingerprint Detect a server impersonation (trust on first use, like SSH) Stored as a SHA-256 hash, per host and port.

These saved records are not uploaded to a publisher-operated server or storage service, and the app's data is excluded from Android backups. The credentials needed to log in are sent to your chosen computer during a connection, as described in section 3.

3. Information sent to your own computer

The purpose of the app is to control a computer you own or administer. While connected, the app sends to that computer, over the Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP, encrypted with TLS and authenticated with NLA):

  • your credentials (to log in),
  • your pointer position and clicks,
  • your keystrokes (physical or virtual keyboard),
  • clipboard text when you use the app's clipboard and paste features.

It receives and processes the screen image, audio and clipboard text supplied by that computer to display the desktop, play its sound and support copying text. The app does not record the desktop image or audio. This traffic goes directly between your headset and your computer, usually on your local network. It does not pass through any server operated by us. What your computer does with this information is under your control.

4. Permissions used

  • Network (Internet): required to open the RDP connection to your computer.
  • Hand tracking: lets you point and click with your hands. The Meta system and the app process hand input locally to drive the pointer. The app does not record or store raw hand tracking data. The resulting pointer actions are sent to your computer, as described in section 3.
  • Passthrough: displays the room around the desktop. Camera images are handled by the Meta system; the app has no access to them and does not store them.
  • Controllers and headset tracking: the app uses system-provided controller models, positions and input to display the scene, place the panels and control the pointer. Raw tracking data is processed on the headset, not recorded or uploaded to us. Controller actions can produce the pointer and keyboard commands sent to your computer.
  • Audio playback: the app plays the sound of the remote computer through its own audio stream. The volume controls in the app change only that stream, through a gain applied inside the app; they never change the headset's system volume, and no permission is needed for them.

The app does not use the microphone, contacts or geographic location, and it does not access your personal files or transfer files to the remote computer.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use cookies. We do not build profiles and we do not sell data.

6. Meta platform, in-app purchase and your Meta user ID

The app runs on Meta Horizon OS and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta processes data about your use of the headset and of the store according to its own policies, which are independent of this app. See the Meta Privacy Policy and the Meta Quest Supplemental Privacy Policy.

Data-handling commitments for Ultra: the following commitments govern the collection and processing of data covered by our User ID (USER_ID) and In-app purchases and/or downloadable content (IAP) feature request through Meta's Data Use Checkup (DUC). Their sole purpose is the one-time Ultra add-on purchase integration.

For Ultra, the User ID (USER_ID) and In-App Purchases (IAP) feature request covers the collection and processing of the numeric Meta user ID and this app's purchase records through the Horizon Platform SDK, solely to verify ownership, unlock Ultra, restore access after reinstalling and refresh the local unlock state. The data-handling commitments are: processing in app memory on the headset, no upload to a publisher-operated server, and persistent storage for Ultra limited to a yes/no "Ultra unlocked" flag without account or purchase identifiers. No Meta name, username or profile picture is read for this purpose. Deletion requests are available to all users, free of charge, by email to bytestudio.rdp@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", with a written response within 30 days. Local app data can also be removed by clearing the app's data in headset settings or uninstalling it.

7. Sharing and retention

The app exchanges RDP data with your chosen computer as described in section 3. For Ultra, the account and purchase requests covered by this policy are exchanged with Meta as described in section 6. We do not sell or rent these data, and do not provide them to advertisers or analytics services. Website analytics are separate and are described in section 12.

Saved connection information stays on the headset until you remove it. Remote desktop image and audio buffers are used for the live session, not saved as recordings. The retention limits for Meta account and purchase data are set out in section 6. We delete data under our control when it is no longer needed for the stated purpose, in response to a valid deletion request, or when required by Meta's policies or applicable law. Section 8 explains what we can delete and which records are controlled by Meta or your own computer.

8. Your choices, and deleting your data

Data deletion request. Write to bytestudio.rdp@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request. This procedure is available to all users, in every country, free of charge. You may use it to request deletion or correction of connection information, Meta user ID or purchase data processed by the app, and any personal information you have sent us in support messages. We answer within 30 days and explain in writing what was deleted, what requires action on your headset, and any records we cannot delete and why.

Tell us that your request concerns ByteStudio Remote Desktop. Do not send your RDP or Meta password, Meta user ID, payment details or desktop content. When you contact us, we receive and process your sender email address and message through our support mailbox solely to answer you and handle the request. We keep this correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not maintain a publisher-side database of app users, Meta IDs or purchases. We cannot remotely erase the private storage of your headset. We will check for any information we hold, delete it where applicable, and guide you through removing local app data. If there is no publisher-held record to delete, we will confirm that explicitly rather than treating your request as unavailable.

On your headset, you can also delete everything the app stores, at any time:

  • Untick Remember password and reconnect to delete the saved password.
  • Clear the app's data from the headset settings (Settings → Apps → ByteStudio Remote Desktop → Clear data) to erase the host, username, password, certificate fingerprints and the "Ultra unlocked" flag.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the headset.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete your Meta account, Meta's purchase records or platform-managed caches, and does not cancel or refund an add-on. Requests concerning records held by Meta must be made to Meta through the privacy and account controls linked in section 6; we cannot delete those records on Meta's behalf. Data held by your remote computer must be removed on that computer. Using the Ultra purchase feature for another ownership check can obtain account and purchase data again from Meta; clearing local data does not delete the source records held by Meta.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your rights under the GDPR remain intact: you may ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and you may object to its processing. Use the address above; we answer within one month.

12. Product website analytics

This Privacy Policy page loads no analytics or tracking script. Reading it does not require an account, login, form submission or consent to analytics. Ordinary requests to the website hosting provider are still necessary to serve the page; the absence of a tracker does not mean that no technical network data reaches that provider.

When enabled, other pages of this website use Umami Cloud to measure page views, visits, referral sources, browser and device types, approximate location, and clicks on the Meta Horizon Store and APK links. A click does not tell us whether you installed the app or bought Ultra. Website analytics do not change the app's data handling.

Umami hosts the analytics data. It does not use tracking cookies or track visitors across websites. We do not send names, email addresses or app connection information to it. As with any web request, the service receives technical information such as an IP address and browser headers to process the request. The tracker excludes the current page's query string and URL fragment. The free plan retains analytics data for six months. See Umami's Privacy Policy for more information.

Analytics are disabled in local previews and when your browser sends an enabled Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. You can also block the analytics script with your browser's content blocker. For questions about website analytics, contact us using the address in section 10.
