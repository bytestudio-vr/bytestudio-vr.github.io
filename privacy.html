Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to the ByteStudio Remote Desktop application for Meta Quest (package com.bytestudio.linuxdesktop), published by ByteStudio VR ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles and why. Analytics on this product website are described separately in section 12 and are not part of the app.

In short, for the app: the app processes connection information, remote desktop content and input to connect your headset to the computer that you choose. Saved connection information stays in the app's private storage on your headset. We operate no app backend and do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or analytics. The Ultra platform feature request covers the collection and processing of your numeric Meta user ID (USER_ID) and this app's purchase records (IAP) through the Horizon Platform SDK solely to verify ownership, unlock Ultra, restore access and refresh the local unlock state. Section 6 sets out the data-handling commitments for these requested features. Local processing by the app is still processing of personal data, even without a publisher-operated server. All users can request data deletion as described in section 8.

1. Who is responsible

Cédric Guillot, based in France, publishes the app under the ByteStudio VR name (Meta developer team: byte_studio) and is the data controller responsible for the purposes and means of the app's processing, including the Meta user ID and purchase data described in section 6. Processing on your headset does not remove this responsibility. Contact details and the data deletion procedure are provided in sections 8 and 10.

2. Information that stays on your headset

To make reconnecting easier, the app stores the following information locally, in its private storage:

DataPurposeDetails

These saved records are not uploaded to a publisher-operated server or storage service, and the app's data is excluded from Android backups. The credentials needed to log in are sent to your chosen computer during a connection, as described in section 3.

3. Information sent to your own computer

The purpose of the app is to control a computer you own or administer. While connected, the app sends to that computer, over the Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP, encrypted with TLS and authenticated with NLA):

  • your credentials (to log in),
  • your pointer position and clicks,
  • your keystrokes (physical or virtual keyboard),
  • clipboard text when you use the app's clipboard and paste features.

It receives and processes the screen image, audio and clipboard text supplied by that computer to display the desktop, play its sound and support copying text. The app does not record the desktop image or audio. This traffic goes directly between your headset and your computer, usually on your local network. It does not pass through any server operated by us. What your computer does with this information is under your control.

When you tap Find a PC, the app also connects to Remote Desktop ports on private addresses of your local network, one subnet per active interface, to list hosts you can reach. This happens only on that request: no scan runs at launch or in the background. Results stay in memory for the session and are not stored, transmitted or shared. Closing the list or the connection form cancels the scan.

4. Permissions used

  • Network (Internet): required to open the RDP connection to your computer and, when you ask for it, to look for Remote Desktop hosts on your local network (section 3).
  • Hand tracking: lets you point and click with your hands. The Meta system and the app process hand input locally to drive the pointer. The app does not record or store raw hand tracking data. The resulting pointer actions are sent to your computer, as described in section 3.
  • Passthrough: displays the room around the desktop. Camera images are handled by the Meta system; the app has no access to them and does not store them.
  • Controllers and headset tracking: the app uses system-provided controller models, positions and input to display the scene, place the panels and control the pointer. Raw tracking data is processed on the headset, not recorded or uploaded to us. Controller actions can produce the pointer and keyboard commands sent to your computer.
  • Audio playback: the app plays the sound of the remote computer through its own audio stream. The volume controls in the app change only that stream, through a gain applied inside the app; they never change the headset's system volume, and no permission is needed for them.

The app does not use the microphone, contacts or geographic location, and it does not access your personal files or transfer files to the remote computer.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use cookies. We do not build profiles and we do not sell data.

The app exchanges RDP data with your chosen computer as described in section 3. For Ultra, the account and purchase requests covered by this policy are exchanged with Meta as described in section 6.   • la position de ton pointeur et tes clics ;

Ce trafic passe directement entre ton casque et ton ordinateur, généralement sur ton réseau local, sans transiter par un serveur que nous exploitons. Le traitement de ces informations par ton ordinateur reste sous ton contrôle.

Lorsque tu appuies sur Find a PC, l’application se connecte aussi aux ports Remote Desktop des adresses privées de ton réseau local, sur un seul sous-réseau par interface active, pour lister les hôtes joignables. Cela se produit uniquement à ta demande : aucun scan n’a lieu au lancement ni en arrière-plan. Les résultats restent en mémoire le temps de la session et ne sont ni conservés, ni transmis, ni partagés. Fermer la liste ou le formulaire annule le scan.
