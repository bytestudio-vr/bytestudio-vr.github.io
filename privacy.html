Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to the GNOME Remote Desktop application for Meta Quest (package com.bytestudio.linuxdesktop), published by ByteStudio VR ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles and why.

In short: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server operated by us. It only connects to the computer that you choose, and everything it remembers stays on your headset.

1. Who is responsible

ByteStudio VR is the publisher of the app. Because no data is sent to us, we do not act as a controller of your personal data in the usual sense. You can contact us at the address given in section 10.

2. Information that stays on your headset

To make reconnecting easier, the app stores the following information locally, in its private storage:

DataPurposeDetails
Host address, port, username Pre-fill the connection form Stored in plain form in the app's private storage.
Password Reconnect without retyping Stored only if you tick "Remember password". Encrypted (AES-GCM) with a non-exportable key of the Android Keystore. Untick the box and reconnect to erase it.
Server certificate fingerprint Detect a server impersonation (trust on first use, like SSH) Stored as a SHA-256 hash, per host and port.

This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app, and the app's data is excluded from Android backups.

3. Information sent to your own computer

The purpose of the app is to control a computer you own or administer. While connected, the app sends to that computer, over the Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP, encrypted with TLS and authenticated with NLA):

  • your credentials (to log in),
  • your pointer position and clicks,
  • your keystrokes (physical or virtual keyboard).

It receives the screen image and the audio of that computer. This traffic goes directly between your headset and your computer, usually on your local network. It does not pass through any server operated by us. What your computer does with this information is under your control.

4. Permissions used

  • Network (Internet): required to open the RDP connection to your computer.
  • Hand tracking: lets you point and click with your hands. Hand data is processed on the headset by the Meta system to drive the pointer; the app does not record it, store it or transmit it.
  • Passthrough: displays the room around the desktop. Camera images are handled by the Meta system; the app has no access to them and does not store them.
  • Rendering the controllers: the app displays the controller models the system provides, so that they are visible in the scene. No button or motion data from them leaves the headset.
  • Audio playback: the app plays the sound of the remote computer through its own audio stream. The volume controls in the app change only that stream, through a gain applied inside the app; they never change the headset's system volume, and no permission is needed for them.

The app does not use the microphone, the contacts, the location or the files of your headset.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not use cookies. We do not build profiles and we do not sell data.

6. Meta platform

The app runs on Meta Horizon OS and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may process data about your use of the headset and of the store according to its own policies, which are independent of this app. See the Meta Privacy Policy and the Meta Quest Supplemental Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and retention

We do not share any data, since we do not have any. Locally stored information is kept until you remove it, as described below.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Untick Remember password and reconnect to delete the saved password.
  • Clear the app's data from the headset settings (Settings → Apps → GNOME Remote Desktop → Clear data) to erase the host, username, password and certificate fingerprints.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the headset.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your rights under the GDPR remain intact and you may contact us at any time.

9. Children

The app is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
